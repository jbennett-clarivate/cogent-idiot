Bayes' theorem updates a belief after new evidence. Start with how likely something is to be true, add how reliable your test is, and it tells you the revised chance it's actually true after a positive result.

Example: a condition affects 1% of people (Prior). The test is positive 90% of the time when the condition is present, but also gives a false positive for 5% of healthy people. A positive result is less certain than it feels (see the Updated belief below).
